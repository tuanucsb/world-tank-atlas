Was the Vietnam War Involved in Any Tank Battles? — YES!

Despite Vietnam's reputation as a helicopter and jungle-infantry conflict, over 2,000 tanks fought across Vietnam, Laos, and Cambodia between 1965 and 1975. The North Vietnamese Army (PAVN/NVA) deployed Soviet PT-76 amphibious light tanks, T-34-85s, T-54/55s, and Chinese Type 59s against US Army/USMC M48A3 Pattons, M551 Sheridans, Australian Centurion Mk 5/1s, and South Vietnamese (ARVN) M41 Walker Bulldogs, M24 Chaffees, and M48A3s:

1. Lang Vei (1968) & Ben Het (1969)

In Feb 1968, 13 NVA tanks overran the US Green Beret camp at Lang Vei. On March 3, 1969 at Ben Het, US Army fought the only US-vs-NVA tank-on-tank night duel of the war, destroying 2 PT-76s by firing at their muzzle flashes.

2. Operation Lam Son 719 (1971) & Easter Offensive (1972)

In 1971, ARVN destroyed 6 T-54s & 16 PT-76s in Laos. In April–June 1972 at Dong Ha, Kontum & An Loc, 400+ NVA & tanks clashed with ARVN 20th Tank Regiment M48A3s at 2,800m and the first combat BGM-71 TOW missiles.

3. Battle of Xuan Loc & Fall of Saigon (April 1975)

Five NVA corps (~600 tanks) spearheaded the Ho Chi Minh Campaign. After fierce tank clashes against ARVN M48A3s and M41s at Xuan Loc, NVA and crashed through the gates of Independence Palace on April 30, 1975.
